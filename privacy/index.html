Privacy Policy

Your data, explained clearly.

Effective August 13, 2026

This policy explains how ShiftBridge collects, uses, shares, stores, and deletes information when you use the ShiftBridge mobile application.

Information we collect

  • Account information: email address, name, authentication records, and selected staff role.
  • Workplace information: workplace memberships, invitations, membership status, and missing-workplace requests you submit.
  • Scheduling content: schedules, available shifts, coverage posts, trade requests, notes, approvals, and related workflow history you choose to provide.
  • Notification information: in-app alert history, notification status, and an Expo push token used to deliver device notifications when you permit them.
  • Safety and service information: blocked-user records, administrative review records, and limited security and service logs generated by our infrastructure providers.

Device-only CE Tracker

Continuing education tracker information, including renewal dates, hour targets, course titles, providers, dates, contact hours, categories, credentialing agencies, provider or activity numbers, delivery format, and local renewal history, is stored only on your device using application storage. ShiftBridge does not upload this tracker information to our servers. It is not backed up or synchronized by ShiftBridge and may be lost if you delete the app, clear its storage, or change devices.

How we use information

We use information sent to our service to authenticate users, operate workplace communities, display schedules to eligible coworkers, facilitate shift coverage and trades, deliver alerts, review workplace requests, provide support, prevent abuse, and maintain service security.

How information is shared

Your name, role, workplace memberships, and scheduling content may be visible to eligible users who share the relevant workplace community. We use service providers, including Supabase for authentication and database infrastructure and Expo for application build, delivery, and push-notification services. Apple and Google process information when you download the app or use their platform services. We do not sell personal information or use it for targeted advertising.

Workplace affiliation

Workplace memberships and directory requests are community-reported. ShiftBridge does not verify employment and is not affiliated with, integrated with, sponsored by, or endorsed by listed employers.

No patient information

Do not enter patient names, medical record numbers, protected health information, or other confidential clinical information anywhere in ShiftBridge. Do not enter personal license numbers, employee IDs, home addresses, or similar identifiers in the CE Tracker. ShiftBridge is not intended to store patient data.

Retention and deletion

We retain server-based account data while your account is active and as needed to operate and secure the service. You can permanently delete your account in the app under Account → Delete Account. You may also follow our web deletion instructions. Account-owned profiles, memberships, schedules, posts, requests, alerts, and registered push tokens are deleted. Limited security or workflow records may remain only after identifiers linking them to you are removed. Account deletion also removes CE Tracker information stored by ShiftBridge on that device.

Notifications

You may decline notification permission or disable notifications in your device settings. Disabling device notifications does not remove in-app alert history. Account deletion removes registered push tokens and account-linked alert records.

Security

We use reasonable technical and organizational safeguards, but no online service or local device storage can guarantee absolute security. Keep your login credentials and device secure and notify us if you suspect unauthorized access.

Children

ShiftBridge is intended for working professionals and is not directed to children under 18.

Your choices

You may decline notification permission, decline to provide optional scheduling content, delete individual calendar or CE Tracker entries, leave workplace memberships when eligible, or delete your entire account. Contact us to ask questions about your information.

Changes

We may update this policy as the service changes. The effective date above identifies the current version.
