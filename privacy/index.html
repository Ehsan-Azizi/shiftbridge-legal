Privacy Policy

Your data, explained clearly.

Effective August 3, 2026

This policy explains how ShiftBridge collects, uses, shares, and deletes information when you use the ShiftBridge mobile application.

Information we collect

  • Account information: email address, name, authentication records, and selected staff role.
  • Community information: the workplace you select and your community membership.
  • Scheduling content: shifts, availability, trade and swap requests, notes, and workflow history you choose to provide.
  • Technical information: limited security and service logs generated by our infrastructure providers.

How we use information

We use this information to authenticate users, operate workplace communities, display schedules to eligible coworkers, facilitate shift matches and swaps, provide support, prevent abuse, and maintain service security.

How information is shared

Your name, role, selected workplace, and scheduling content may be visible to eligible users in the same workplace community. We use service providers, including Supabase for authentication and database infrastructure and Expo for application build and delivery services. Apple and Google process information when you download or use their distribution services. We do not sell personal information or use it for targeted advertising.

Workplace affiliation

Workplace selections are community-reported. ShiftBridge does not verify employment and is not affiliated with or endorsed by listed employers.

No patient information

Do not enter patient names, medical record numbers, protected health information, or other confidential clinical information. ShiftBridge is not intended to store patient data.

Retention and deletion

We retain account data while your account is active and as needed to operate and secure the service. You can permanently delete your account in the app under Profile → Delete account. You may also follow our web deletion instructions. Account-owned schedules, posts, trades, and swaps are deleted; limited workflow records may be retained only in anonymized form.

Security

We use reasonable technical and organizational safeguards, but no online service can guarantee absolute security. Keep your login credentials private and notify us if you suspect unauthorized access.

Children

ShiftBridge is intended for working professionals and is not directed to children under 18.

Your choices

You may decline to provide optional scheduling content, delete individual calendar entries, or delete your entire account. Contact us to ask questions about your information.

Changes

We may update this policy as the service changes. The effective date above identifies the current version.
