Privacy Policy

Your data, explained clearly.

Effective August 22, 2026

This policy explains how ShiftBridge collects, uses, shares, stores, and deletes information when you use the ShiftBridge mobile application. “ShiftBridge,” “we,” and “us” refer to the operator identified in Contact below.

Information we collect

  • Account information: email address, name, account identifier, authentication records, and the date you accept our Terms.
  • Workplace and role information: workforce category, workplace type, selected workplace, Area, approved role, custom role requests, invitations, membership status, and administrative approval decisions.
  • Scheduling and user content: schedule entries, shifts, giveaways, swaps, requests, free-text notes, approval handoffs, and workflow history you submit.
  • Safety and support information: reports, report reasons, blocked-user records, workplace requests, support communications, and limited security and service logs.
  • Notification information: in-app alert history, notification status, platform, and an Expo push token when you enable device notifications. ShiftBridge does not need or store your personal device name for push delivery.
  • Optional nearby search: if you choose “Use my current location,” the app rounds your coordinates to a broad, city-scale area before sending them to our workplace-search service. ShiftBridge uses them only for that search and does not save them to your account.

How we use information

We use information to authenticate users, manage workplace and role approvals, limit access to eligible workplace members, show calendars and trade opportunities to users with the same workplace, Area, and approved role, facilitate shift coordination, deliver alerts, investigate reports, provide support, prevent abuse, and secure the service.

Who can see your information

Your name, approved role, workplace affiliation, schedule times, posts, and requests may be visible to eligible users in the same workplace, Area, and approved role. Administrators may review workplace, role, safety, and support requests. A ShiftBridge match is not employer approval and is not an official schedule change.

Service providers and directory data

We use Supabase for authentication, database, server functions, and service infrastructure; Expo for app delivery and push notifications; and Geoapify for general workplace search using OpenStreetMap-derived directory data. When optional nearby search is used, Geoapify may process the rounded location, search terms, IP address, request headers, and timestamp under its privacy terms. Apple and Google process information when you download the app or use their platform services. We do not sell personal information, use it for targeted advertising, or track users across unrelated apps or websites.

Workplace affiliation and directory listings

Workplace memberships, roles, Area selections, and directory requests are community-reported. Approval grants access inside ShiftBridge only. It does not verify employment, credentials, licensing, authorization, or employer endorsement. Directory information may originate from public third-party data and may be inaccurate or incomplete.

Location choices

Nearby search is optional. You may deny location permission and search by workplace name, city, state, or ZIP instead. ShiftBridge does not use background location and does not display your location to other users.

Do not enter sensitive information

Do not enter patient, customer, passenger, student, medical, financial, government identifier, employee ID, license number, home address, password, confidential employer information, or other sensitive or regulated information in notes, schedules, role requests, reports, or support messages. ShiftBridge is not designed to store protected health information or employer-confidential records.

Retention and deletion

We retain account and active-workflow information while your account is active. Open scheduling content is retained while needed to provide the service; completed workflow history may remain available for operational continuity and dispute handling. Safety reports and security logs may be retained for a reasonable period to investigate abuse, enforce our Terms, prevent fraud, and satisfy legal obligations. We periodically review retained data and remove or de-identify information that is no longer reasonably necessary.

You can permanently delete your account in the app under Account → Delete Account or use our web deletion instructions. Deletion removes your authentication account and account-linked profile, memberships, schedules, posts, requests, alerts, push tokens, role requests, blocks, and user content unless retention is legally required. Limited security or workflow records may remain only after identifiers linking them to you are removed.

Your choices and requests

You may deny location and notification permissions, edit or delete supported content, leave a workplace when eligible, block users, or delete your account. You may contact us to request access to or correction or deletion of your information. We may need to verify account ownership before completing a request.

Security

We use reasonable technical and organizational safeguards, including access controls and encrypted network connections, but no system can guarantee absolute security. Keep your credentials and device secure and report suspected unauthorized access.

Children

ShiftBridge is a workforce coordination service and is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. Contact us if you believe a child has provided information so we can investigate and delete it.

Changes

We may update this policy as the service changes. We will update the effective date and provide additional notice when required.

Contact

For privacy, account, or support questions, contact ShiftBridge at shiftbridgesupport@gmail.com.
