Account deletion

Delete your ShiftBridge account.

Delete immediately in the app

  1. Sign in to ShiftBridge.
  2. Open Account.
  3. Scroll to Delete Account.
  4. Review both confirmations and tap Delete permanently.

This deletes your authentication account, profile, workplace memberships, schedules, shift posts, trade and coverage requests, in-app alerts, registered push tokens, and related account-owned data. It also removes ShiftBridge CE Tracker information stored for your account on that device. Limited security or workflow history may remain only after identifiers linking it to you are removed.

Request deletion without app access

If you cannot sign in, email us from the address associated with your ShiftBridge account. Use the subject “Delete my ShiftBridge account.” We may request limited information to confirm account ownership before deletion.

Request account deletion

Do not include your password, verification code, nursing license number, employee ID, patient information, certificate, transcript, or confidential clinical information.

Device-only information

If you no longer have access to the device, deleting the app or clearing its app storage removes device-only CE Tracker records. ShiftBridge cannot remotely retrieve or delete information that was never uploaded to our service.
