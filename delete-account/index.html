Account deletion

Delete your ShiftBridge account.

Delete immediately in the app

  1. Sign in to ShiftBridge.
  2. Open the Profile tab.
  3. Tap Delete account.
  4. Review both confirmations and tap Delete permanently.

This deletes your authentication account, profile, memberships, schedules, shift posts, trade requests, and swap requests. Limited workflow history may remain only after identifiers linking it to you are removed.

Request deletion without app access

If you cannot sign in, email us from the address associated with your ShiftBridge account. Use the subject “Delete my ShiftBridge account.” We may request limited information to confirm account ownership before deletion.

Request account deletion

Do not include your password, verification code, patient information, or confidential clinical information.
