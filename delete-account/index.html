Account deletion

Delete your ShiftBridge account.

Delete immediately in the app

  1. Sign in to ShiftBridge.
  2. Open Account.
  3. Scroll to Delete Account.
  4. Review both confirmations and tap Delete permanently.

This deletes your authentication account and account-linked profile, workplace memberships, role requests, schedules, shift posts, giveaway and swap requests, alerts, push tokens, blocks, reports, and related user content. Limited security or workflow records may remain only when legally necessary or after identifiers linking them to you are removed.

Request deletion without app access

If you cannot sign in, email us from the address associated with your account with the subject “Delete my ShiftBridge account.” We may request limited information to confirm ownership before deletion.

Request account deletion

Do not include your password, verification code, employee or license number, patient or customer information, government identifier, home address, or confidential employer information.
