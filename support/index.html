Support

How can we help?

For sign-in problems, incorrect workplace access, safety concerns, notifications, CE Tracker questions, or other ShiftBridge issues, email our support contact.

Email ShiftBridge support

Include in your message

  • A short description of the issue.
  • Your device type and app version, if known.
  • Steps that led to the problem.
Never email your password, verification code, nursing license number, employee ID, patient information, certificate, transcript, or confidential clinical information. ShiftBridge support will never ask for your password.

CE Tracker support

CE Tracker records are stored only on your device and are not uploaded, backed up, or synchronized by ShiftBridge. Support cannot retrieve lost tracker records. Keep official continuing education certificates and compliance documentation separately.

Notification support

If device alerts are unavailable, check notification permission for ShiftBridge in your device settings. In-app alerts may remain available even when device notifications are disabled.

Account deletion

You can delete your account immediately in the app under Account → Delete Account. See account deletion instructions for alternatives.
