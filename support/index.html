Support

How can we help?

For sign-in problems, incorrect workplace or role access, location search, safety concerns, notifications, or other ShiftBridge issues, email our support contact.

Email ShiftBridge support

Include in your message

  • A short description of the issue.
  • Your device type and app version, if known.
  • Steps that led to the problem.
  • The workplace and Area name only when needed to investigate access.
Never email your password, verification code, employee or license number, patient or customer information, government identifier, home address, screenshot containing private data, or confidential employer information. ShiftBridge support will never ask for your password.

Safety reports

Use the separate Report and Block actions in the app when another user or post is objectionable. Reports are sent for administrative review; blocking immediately hides supported interactions from that user. For immediate danger, contact local emergency services or your employer.

Workplace and role approvals

A pending workplace or custom role remains unavailable for calendar and trading access until an administrator reviews it. Approval grants ShiftBridge access only and does not verify employment, credentials, licensing, or employer authorization.

Location and notifications

Nearby workplace search is optional. If location is denied, include a city, state, or ZIP in your search. If device alerts are unavailable, check notification permission in device settings; in-app alerts may still be available.

Privacy requests

Email us to request access to, correction of, or deletion of your personal information. Contact us from the email associated with your account when possible. We may request limited information to verify ownership, but never your password.

Account deletion

You can delete your account immediately in the app under Account → Delete Account. See account deletion instructions if you cannot sign in.
