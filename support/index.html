Support

How can we help?

For sign-in problems, incorrect workplace access, safety concerns, or other ShiftBridge questions, email our support contact.

Email ShiftBridge support

Include in your message

  • A short description of the issue.
  • Your device type and app version, if known.
  • Steps that led to the problem.
Never email your password, verification code, patient information, or confidential clinical information. ShiftBridge support will never ask for your password.

Account deletion

You can delete your account immediately in the app under Profile → Delete account. See account deletion instructions for alternatives.
